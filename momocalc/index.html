1. Core Commitment & Offline Architecture

MomoCalc is a fast, standalone calculator utility for estimating mobile money charges and agent cash-out tariffs (such as Safaricom M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, and Airtel Money). It does not require user registration, does not create an online profile, and performs calculations locally without connecting to external servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Financial Data: We never have access to, log, or transmit transaction amounts, payment cards, bank accounts, or wallet credentials;
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone contacts, or physical GPS locations;
  • No User Account: The application is fully functional without any login or authentication credentials.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To maintain MomoCalc free for all users worldwide, the App displays ads powered by Google AdMob (Google Play Services). Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous technical device telemetry according to Google's standard privacy policies:

  • Mobile Advertising ID (AAID);
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system build;
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, display duration, and clicks).

Users can reset or limit ad personalization at any time through their Android system settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset advertising ID.

For additional details on Google's advertising data practices, consult How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Device Permissions & Local Storage

MomoCalc requests only minimal standard Android operating system permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required exclusively to retrieve and display advertisements via Google AdMob;
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify network connectivity before requesting ad banners;
  • Local Preferences (SharedPreferences): Used solely on your device to remember your last selected telecom operator (e.g., Safaricom Kenya or MTN Uganda). This setting never leaves your phone.

5. Data Retention & User Data Deletion

Because MomoCalc operates on a 100% offline-first architecture with zero personal accounts and zero backend cloud storage:

  • No Server Retention: The Developer retains no personal records, transaction amounts, calculations, or financial data on any server;
  • Local Device Data Deletion: The only stored information is local device UI preferences (such as your last chosen operator or country code) saved in Android SharedPreferences. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings > Apps > MomoCalc > Storage > Clear Data, or by simply uninstalling the application from your device.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The application is a financial utility designed for adult mobile money calculations and is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
