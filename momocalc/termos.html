2. Scope & Utility Nature of the Application

MomoCalc is an educational and mathematical utility calculator engineered to estimate mobile money transaction fees, reverse cash-out costs, agent withdrawal charges, and applicable government taxes (e.g., excise duties, statutory levies) for mobile money networks operating primarily in East and West Africa (including Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, and Ghana).

The core feature of the App allows senders to calculate the exact gross amount they must transfer so that the recipient receives the intended net amount after the operator's agent withdrawal fee has been deducted.
